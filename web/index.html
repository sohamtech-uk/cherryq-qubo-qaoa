Why quantum?

Not because five invoices are hard.

Five invoices are our verifiable laboratory. The research question starts when binary choices, dependencies and receipt scenarios scale together.

Our hypothesis

Payment decisions are binary, supplier dependencies are quadratic, and cash constraints map naturally to QUBO.

  • Invoice = binary decision
  • Dependency = quadratic interaction
  • Budget + timing = penalties
Classical optimisation remains the benchmark — and may remain better for many instances.
