Why quantum, honestly?

Not to replace MILP at 10 invoices.

The plausible crossover is a rolling enterprise treasury problem where classical presolve leaves a tightly connected hard core and a decision is needed before MILP closes its optimality gap.

Thousands of invoices→Classical presolve→50–150 contested decisions→Hybrid QAOA

Future success criterion

Under an equal end-to-end deadline, CherryQ must repeatedly return a lower-loss feasible plan than tuned Gurobi, CP-SAT and SCIP on unseen hard instances.

  • same objective and constraints
  • all classical + quantum overhead included
  • time-to-good-solution, not only proof time
  • statistical evidence across instance families
